About Jalgaon Zone

Jalgaon Zone is a local information platform focused on Jalgaon district and Khandesh. We publish information related to Jalgaon news, agriculture, education, jobs, government schemes, history, tourism, local places, weather and practical information.

Our goal is to make useful Jalgaon-related information easier to discover in one place. Readers should verify time-sensitive information with the relevant official source before making important decisions.
