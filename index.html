About Jalgaon Zone

जळगाव जिल्ह्यासाठी उपयुक्त माहिती एकाच ठिकाणी.

Jalgaon Zone is a local information platform focused on Jalgaon district and Khandesh. We publish information related to Jalgaon news, agriculture, education, jobs, government schemes, history, tourism, local places and practical information.

Our goal is to make useful Jalgaon-related information easier to discover in one place. Readers should verify time-sensitive information with the relevant official source before making important decisions.
