About Jalgaon

Jalgaon is a famous district of Maharashtra known for agriculture, bananas, culture, history and religious places. The district is popularly associated with banana production and is often called the Banana Capital of India.

Jalgaon History

Jalgaon is part of the historic Khandesh region. The area has an important cultural and historical background. The famous Faizpur session of the Indian National Congress was held in 1936.
