Jalgaon History

Know the story behind the city and region

🏛️ Historic Khandesh

Jalgaon is part of the historic Khandesh region. The area has an important cultural and historical background. The famous Faizpur session of the Indian National Congress was held in 1936.

🌾 Agriculture & Bananas

Jalgaon is well known for agriculture and banana production. The district is popularly associated with banana cultivation and is often called the Banana Capital of India.
