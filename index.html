KNOW JALGAON

Why is Jalgaon Famous?

Jalgaon is a district in Maharashtra known for agriculture, culture, historical places and religious destinations.

It is especially well known for banana cultivation and is popularly associated with the title "Banana Capital of India."

The region also has places such as Unapdev, Padmalaya, Manudevi, Patanadevi, Parola Fort and the Swinging Towers of Farkande.

🍌 Did You Know?

Jalgaon is famous for its banana cultivation and agricultural economy.

🏛️ History of Jalgaon

Jalgaon has a rich historical background connected with the wider Khandesh region of Maharashtra.

The region has experienced the influence of different kingdoms and political powers over centuries, contributing to its diverse cultural heritage.

Jalgaon also has an important connection with India's freedom movement. Faizpur in Jalgaon district hosted the Indian National Congress session in 1936.
